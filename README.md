## 💡 My Journey: From Recruitment to Data Engineering  

I initially started my career as an **IT Recruiter**, but my fascination with **data and analytics** led me to transition into data engineering. Looking back, I realized that no matter what profession we work in, we all engage in some form of **analysis**.  

Take recruitment, for example:  
- 📊 **Data-Driven Decisions:** Understanding market trends, candidate skills, and job requirements is **no different from analyzing datasets**.  
- 🎯 **Storytelling:** In analytics, we derive insights from data; in recruitment, we break down information and present it meaningfully to both **clients** and **candidates**.  

This shift fueled my passion for **solving data challenges**, and today, I work with **Apache Spark, PySpark, Azure Databricks, Snowflake, and Python** to build **high-performance data solutions**.
