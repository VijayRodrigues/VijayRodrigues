### 💡 My Journey: From Recruitment to Data Engineering

I started my career as an **IT Recruiter**, but my fascination with **data and analytics** led me to transition into data engineering.  
Looking back, I realized that no matter what profession we work in, we all engage in some form of **analysis**.

#### 🎯 Lessons from Recruitment:
- 📊 **Data-Driven Decisions**: Understanding market trends, candidate skills, and job requirements is no different from analyzing datasets.  
- 📢 **Storytelling**: In analytics, we derive insights from data; in recruitment, we present information meaningfully to both **clients** and **candidates**.

This shift fueled my passion for **solving data challenges**, and today I work with tools like **Apache Spark, PySpark, Azure Databricks, Snowflake, and Python** to build high-performance, scalable data solutions.
